# Fee Schedule (2026)

## Annual fees

The Everyday Card has no annual fee. The Voyager Card annual fee is $125, charged on the first statement after account opening and each anniversary after that. Voyager cardmembers who downgrade to the Everyday Card within 30 days of the annual fee posting receive a full refund of that fee.

## Late payment fee

If the minimum payment is not received by the due date, a late fee of $32 applies. A second late payment within the next six billing cycles raises the fee to $41. No late fee is charged when the minimum payment is $10 or less.

## Foreign transaction fee

The Everyday Card charges 2.7% of each purchase made in a foreign currency or processed outside the United States. The Voyager Card has no foreign transaction fee.

## Returned payment fee

A returned payment fee of $29 applies when a payment is returned unpaid by the cardmember's bank. Only one returned payment fee is charged per billing cycle.

## Card replacement

Standard replacement cards are free. Expedited delivery of a replacement card costs $15, and the fee is waived when the card was reported lost or stolen.
