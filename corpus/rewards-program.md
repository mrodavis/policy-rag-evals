# Harborline Rewards Program

## Earning points

Everyday cardmembers earn 1 point per dollar on all purchases. Voyager cardmembers earn 3 points per dollar on travel and dining and 1 point per dollar on everything else. Balance transfers, cash advances and fees do not earn points.

## Redeeming points

Points can be redeemed for statement credits at 1 cent per point, or for travel booked through the Harborline travel portal at 1.25 cents per point for Voyager cardmembers. The minimum redemption is 2,500 points.

## Expiration and closing

Points do not expire while the account is open and in good standing. If the account is closed by the cardmember, points must be redeemed within 30 days of closing or they are forfeited. Points are forfeited immediately if the account is closed for fraud.
