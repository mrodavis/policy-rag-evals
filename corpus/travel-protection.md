# Voyager Travel Protections

## Trip delay reimbursement

When a covered trip is delayed by more than 6 hours, Voyager cardmembers can be reimbursed up to $300 per ticket for meals, lodging and toiletries. The trip must have been paid for with the Voyager Card. Claims must be filed within 60 days of the delay.

## Lost luggage

Checked bags that the carrier declares lost are covered up to $1,500 per traveler, minus any amount the carrier pays. Cash, jewelry and electronics are excluded.

## Rental car coverage

Rental car damage coverage is secondary in the United States and primary outside the United States. The cardmember must decline the rental company's collision damage waiver and pay for the full rental with the Voyager Card.

## Not covered

Travel protections do not cover trips booked with points from another program, trips longer than 60 days, or delays caused by a known weather event announced before the trip was booked.
