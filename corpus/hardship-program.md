# Financial Hardship Program

## Eligibility

Cardmembers who have had a job loss, medical emergency, natural disaster or a similar financial hardship can ask to enroll. The account must be open and no more than 90 days past due.

## What the program offers

Enrolled cardmembers receive a reduced APR of 9.9% for 12 months, and late fees are waived while they are enrolled. Card use is suspended during enrollment and resumes after six consecutive on-time payments.

## How agents enroll a cardmember

Agents must not promise enrollment. Agents collect the reason for the hardship and transfer the call to the Customer Assistance team, which makes the decision within 5 business days.
