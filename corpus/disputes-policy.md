# Billing Disputes Policy

## Who can dispute and when

A cardmember can dispute a charge within 60 days of the statement date on which the charge first appeared. Authorized users cannot open disputes; the primary cardmember must call or use the app.

## What happens after a dispute is opened

Harborline issues a temporary credit for the disputed amount within 2 business days. The temporary credit stays on the account while the dispute is investigated. Most investigations close within 45 days, and no investigation stays open longer than 90 days.

## Merchant response

The merchant has 20 days to respond with evidence. If the merchant does not respond in time, the dispute is decided in the cardmember's favor and the temporary credit becomes permanent.

## Disputes decided for the merchant

If the evidence supports the merchant, the temporary credit is reversed and the cardmember is notified in writing with a copy of the merchant's evidence. Interest that would have accrued on the disputed amount during the investigation is not charged.

## Fraud versus disputes

Charges the cardmember did not make at all are fraud, not disputes. Agents must transfer fraud claims to the Fraud team and close the card immediately rather than opening a dispute.
