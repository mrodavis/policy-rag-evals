# Lost or Stolen Cards

## Reporting

Cardmembers can lock a card instantly in the app and report it lost or stolen in the app or by phone at any time. A new card number is issued as soon as the card is reported.

## Liability

Cardmembers have $0 liability for unauthorized charges made after the card is reported lost or stolen, and for any unauthorized charges reported within 60 days of the statement on which they appear.

## Replacement delivery

Replacement cards arrive in 5 to 7 business days by standard mail. Expedited delivery in 2 business days is available at no cost for lost or stolen cards.
