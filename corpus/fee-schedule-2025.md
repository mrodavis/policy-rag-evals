# Fee Schedule (2025)

## Annual fees

The Everyday Card has no annual fee. The Voyager Card annual fee is $95.

## Late payment fee

A late fee of $29 applies if the minimum payment is not received by the due date, rising to $40 for a second late payment within six billing cycles.

## Foreign transaction fee

The Everyday Card charges 3% of each foreign purchase. The Voyager Card has no foreign transaction fee.
